# Closer Copilot — one page

Live AI coaching during a sales call. It whispers the next line to the closer — the exact words
and how to say them — about a second after the prospect stops talking. It works from your own
playbook and pricing, remembers every previous call with that prospect, and refuses to say
anything it can't source. Nothing joins the meeting.

## USPs

| | What it does | How it helps on the call |
|---|---|---|
| **Live, not after** | ~1s to first words, 1.2–1.4s to the full line, streamed | The objection lands, you glance down, the line is there. No dead air. |
| **Invisible** | Audio off your mic + browser tab. No bot, no participant, no consent prompt | Prospects talk straight when nothing is visibly recording. No IT approval either. |
| **No integration** | Share your screen. Video call, dialer, WhatsApp, phone on speaker | Usable on the call you have in twenty minutes. |
| **Tells you HOW to say it** | Pause marks, falling pitch on certainty, rising on questions, the one word to stress, when to soften | Tonality is most of the sale. A statement said like a question gets treated like one. |
| **Can't fabricate** | Every number checked against your product knowledge, deal history and the live transcript. Can't source it → says nothing | You read the card out loud without checking it. Caught inventing a client result that didn't exist. |
| **Does the math** | Their stated figures calculated **in code**, handed over as a line: *"45 calls a week is ~180 a month; two closing at nine grand is eighteen thousand walking out"* | The strongest move in the room, in their own numbers — nobody can do that arithmetic live while listening. |
| **Remembers the deal** | Objections, commitments, stakeholders, pain carried call to call | Deals are won across conversations. Stops you re-asking what they already told you. |
| **Knows who spoke** | Mic and prospect on two separate channels — separation is structural, not guessed | Anything that joins a meeting gets one mixed stream and infers the speaker. Wrong guess = nonsense card. |
| **Your voice** | Your pricing, your objection answers, your phrases, a "never say" list, separate playbook per offer | "Handle the price objection" isn't a line. *Your* price and *your* anchor is. |
| **Plus** | Call goal (discovery / qualify / close), live checklist of what's still uncovered, post-call review with talk ratio and monologue length, voice practice mode | |

## What Cluely doesn't do
*Category positioning — not independently verified. Don't quote specifics at a prospect.*
- Doesn't know your offer, pricing or playbook — plausible sentences, never *your* sentence
- No grounding layer — nothing stops it stating a price or result that doesn't exist
- No memory of the deal; call two starts from zero
- No delivery coaching, and not built on a sales methodology

## What Abstrakt doesn't do
*The closest real-time competitor. A good product for contact centres.*
- **Doesn't work without an integration** — HubSpot, SalesLoft, Five9, Genesys, RingCX, configured per customer during onboarding
- **Won't say anything a manager didn't pre-approve** — surfaces approved scripts only, no independent reasoning. The objection nobody anticipated has no card behind it
- **No delivery coaching** — words, not tone
- **No cross-call memory** — logs notes to a CRM. That's a record, not a memory
- **Sold to the manager**, per seat, for a team

*Where they're genuinely ahead — say it first:* dialer integrations, compliance tracking, manager
dashboards. All of it serves a call-centre floor. If that's what you're running, buy theirs.

## Everyone else
- **Gong** — scores the call after it ends. Different moment; people run both
- **Nooks / Attention / Balto** — same shape as Abstrakt, sold to managers
- **CloserCoach.ai** — roleplay and post-call scoring, not live. No cross-call memory
- **Hyperbound Perform** — manager tooling on Gong/Salesloft, for rescuing deals from a dashboard
- **Cirrus Insight** — pre-call briefing, requires Salesforce
- **Hebbrix** — context loading, async not live

## The three things nobody else combines

1. **Live suggestions that are reasoned, not retrieved** — generated for the actual moment, not pulled from a script a manager approved in advance.
2. **Generative that still can't lie** — scripted tools are safe but rigid; generative tools are flexible but fabricate. This is the only one that gets both instead of trading one for the other.
3. **Memory across the whole deal** — not one call at a time.

For someone with no CRM, no manager and no script, closing their own leads.

> Nearly everything in this category is sold to a manager watching or a coach reviewing.
> Gong scores you. Hyperbound flags you. Abstrakt keeps you on someone else's script.
> This is the only one built for the person actually talking, while they're talking.
