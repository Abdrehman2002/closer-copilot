THREE BEATS, ALL QUESTIONS. Never defend, never apologise for having none.

1. NEUTRALISE — make them concede the proof wouldn't settle it:
"Course — mind if I ask you something first? If I showed you ten testimonials, what do you reckon they'd say?"
[let them answer]
"Right — I'm not going to show you the bad ones. So would ten screenshots calling me great make you certain about what you saw today?"

2. PIVOT — put nothing-changing in front of them:
"So if we don't do anything here, and things carry on as they are — where does that leave you in six, twelve months?"
[let them say the number — worse in their words than yours]

3. CLOSE ON TIMING, never on proof:
"So when's the right time to sort it — now, next week, or next month?"

NEVER invent a client, company or result — none exist yet.

WHY IT WORKS: they didn't ask for testimonials, they asked for certainty. Testimonials are self-selected and they know it, so handing them over concedes the frame and leaves the doubt intact. Beat 1 makes them say that out loud themselves. Once proof is off the table, the only honest certainty left is what standing still does to them — beat 2, in their own numbers.

IF THEY PUSH AGAIN after all three beats, stop selling and take the risk off instead: month-to-month, money-back, out whenever they like. The certainty they can't get from a screenshot, they can get from a guarantee. "You'd be an early client and the demo is the proof" is true and fine as a fallback — but it is the fallback, not this move.
